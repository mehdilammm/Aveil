Morocco has a vibrant artisan culture, and several online platforms have emerged to connect local craftspeople with buyers. Whether you are looking for traditional Berber rugs, handmade leather goods, organic argan oil, or contemporary Moroccan fashion, here are the best online stores for Moroccan handmade and local products:

### 1. Dedicated Moroccan Artisan Marketplaces

*   **Chabi Chic:** 
    *   *What they sell:* Contemporary tableware, ceramics, kitchenware, and natural cosmetics.
    *   *Why it’s good:* They take traditional Moroccan craftsmanship and give it a modern, chic twist. Everything is designed in-house and made by local artisans. They ship both locally within Morocco and internationally.
*   **Anou (The Anou Cooperative):** 
    *   *What they sell:* Authentic rugs, woodwork, metalwork, and textiles.
    *   *Why it’s good:* The Anou is a unique, artisan-owned platform. It operates as a cooperative, meaning 100% of the profits go directly to the artisans (often women’s cooperatives in remote areas). It’s the most ethical way to buy authentic Moroccan crafts online.
*   **Moroccan Epicerie / Local Cooperatives:**
    *   *What they sell:* Culinary products like Amlou (argan, almond, and honey spread), organic argan oil, rose water, spices, and local honey.
    *   *Why it’s good:* Great for foodies looking for certified authentic, locally sourced Moroccan terroir products. 

### 2. Curated Boutiques & Designer Brands

*   **MTAAR (Made in Morocco):**
    *   *What they sell:* A wide variety of products ranging from fashion and shoes to home decor and accessories.
    *   *Why it’s good:* It acts as a showcase for independent Moroccan designers and creators, making it a great one-stop shop for modern Moroccan lifestyle goods.
*   **Lalla and Mint:**
    *   *What they sell:* Leather bags, poufs, baskets, and travel accessories.
    *   *Why it’s good:* Known for high-quality craftsmanship, blending traditional Moroccan materials (like cactus silk and leather) with modern, functional designs.

### 3. Global Platforms with a Strong Moroccan Presence

*   **Etsy:**
    *   While not a Moroccan site specifically, Etsy is arguably the biggest online marketplace for Moroccan handmade goods. Many Moroccan artisans, rug dealers, and leather workers ship directly from Fez, Marrakech, and Casablanca. 
    *   *Tip:* Look for sellers who ship directly from Morocco to ensure authenticity and support local makers.

### 4. Social Media (Instagram & Facebook)

*   Many of Morocco's best independent artisans and young designers don't have traditional websites; instead, they run their businesses entirely through **Instagram**. 
*   Searching hashtags like `#MoroccanCrafts`, `#HandmadeInMorocco`, `#MadeInMorocco`, or `#ArtisanatMarocain` will connect you directly with makers of custom clothing, jewelry, and home decor. Transactions are usually handled via WhatsApp or DM.

---

### Tips for Buying Handmade in Morocco Online:
1.  **Customization:** Many Moroccan artisans are happy to customize sizes (especially for rugs, poufs, and clothing). Don't hesitate to ask.
2.  **Shipping:** If buying directly from local sites, check their international shipping policies, as customs duties may apply depending on your home country.
3.  **Authentication:** For items like argan oil or saffron, look for labels indicating local cooperatives (*coopérative féminine*) to ensure purity and fair trade.
